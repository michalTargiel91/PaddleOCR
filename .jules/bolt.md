## 2025-02-28 - Optimizing Shapely Polygons in PaddleOCR poly_nms
**Learning:** In `ppocr/utils/poly_nms.py`, precomputing Shapely polygons and their areas significantly speeds up Non-Maximum Suppression (from ~40s to ~1.7s for 500 polygons). However, blindly pre-buffering polygons (e.g., `poly.buffer(0.0001)`) adds excessive vertices, making the intersection vastly slower (taking longer than the unoptimized code) and negating the caching benefits.
**Action:** When optimizing Shapely geometric intersections in inner loops, only precompute the base polygon and its area. Intersect the unbuffered polygons in the inner loop (`A.intersection(B)`), and wrap it in a `try...except Exception:` block to gracefully fall back to the original unoptimized helper function (`boundary_iou`) when highly irregular OCR polygons cause `TopologicalError`s in Shapely.
