## 2024-05-23 - Optimize Shapely Polygon Precomputation in NMS
**Learning:** High O(N^2) complexity in nested loops for Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS) can be drastically reduced from over 1.4s to 0.1s by precomputing Shapely `Polygon` objects, their areas, and buffered fallback instances once per polygon. Unconditionally buffering valid polygons dilates geometries and breaks IoU math; strictly apply buffering logic identical to the slow path, and handle `shapely.errors.TopologicalError` to ensure geometric robustness without broad `except Exception` blocks that mask real errors.
**Action:** When working with nested O(N^2) loops in geographic or geometric code (like Shapely), always precompute invariants in a linear O(N) pass before the O(N^2) logic, and map index references to precomputed tuples to minimize expensive constructor and property calls.
