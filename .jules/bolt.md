## 2024-06-15 - Optimize poly_nms with O(N) Geometry Pre-computation
**Learning:** In `poly_nms`, calculating polygon areas and intersections redundantly inside an O(N^2) loop causes massive overhead, especially with Shapely operations like `.buffer()`. Also, unbuffered area calculation can yield 0.0 for degenerate polygons, leading to incorrect IoU or negative calculations when intersected with buffered geometry.
**Action:** Pre-compute Polygon objects, their buffered forms, and their buffered areas in an initial O(N) pass. Wrap this pre-computation in a `try...except Exception` to safely handle degenerate geometries by storing `None`, and handle `None` gracefully in the inner O(N^2) comparison loop. Inline intersection calculations within the inner loop for further overhead reduction.
