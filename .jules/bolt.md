## 2024-05-15 - Precomputing Shapely polygons in poly_nms loop
**Learning:** Found a significant O(n^2) performance bottleneck in PaddleOCR's `poly_nms` function due to repeated instantiation of Shapely `Polygon` objects inside a nested loop for every IoU calculation. Precomputing these objects and their areas ahead of the loop, and inlining the `poly_iou` calculation to skip `boundary_iou` overhead, dramatically speeds up Non-Maximum Suppression (from ~0.68s down to ~0.05s for 100 polygons). When precomputing, invalid geometries must be safely handled since inference results can be malformed, and `Exception` handling around `points2polygon` and `.intersection()` is required to match original fallback behavior.
**Action:** Always check inner loops in NMS algorithms for repeated, heavy geometric operations. Extract object instantiation outside the loops and inline simple arithmetic/intersection properties where possible while preserving safe exception handling for invalid inputs.
