## 2025-02-20 - Pre-computing Shapely Geometry in NMS Loops
**Learning:** Pre-computing `Polygon` objects and their buffered forms in O(N) rather than redundantly inside O(N^2) operations (like `poly_nms`) provides >10x speedup while preserving exact backward compatibility. Doing this requires caching both the buffered form (`poly.buffer(0.0001)`) and the original unbuffered area (`poly.area`) because `poly_union` uses the unbuffered areas of the polygons + the buffered intersection.
**Action:** When optimizing geometry loops, compute geometries/buffers upfront in an $O(N)$ pass. Always wrap the pre-computation itself in a `try...except Exception` block to catch errors from degenerate polygons, storing `None` and handling it safely during downstream downstream IOU comparisons.
