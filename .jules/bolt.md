## 2024-06-28 - Pre-compute Shapely geometries for NMS performance
**Learning:** In PaddleOCR's `poly_nms`, `poly_intersection` and `poly_union` are repeatedly called inside an O(N²) loop. Modifying the signatures of these shared helper functions causes `TypeError`s or massive performance regressions due to indiscriminate buffering.
**Action:** Instead, pre-compute the Polygon shapes, their unbuffered areas, and their buffered forms in O(N) *before* the loop. Wrap the `Polygon` instantiation and `.buffer()` calls in a `try...except Exception` block to catch degenerate polygons and store `None`. Then, inline the intersection area and union logic directly within the `poly_nms` hot loop to process the pre-computed polygons, avoiding function call overhead while precisely handling valid/invalid states and maintaining strict backwards-compatibility (measured 17x speedup).
