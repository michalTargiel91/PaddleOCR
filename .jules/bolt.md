## 2024-06-16 - Pre-computing Geometry Objects and Safe Areas for Poly NMS
**Learning:** When optimizing O(N^2) geometric algorithms like `poly_nms` by pre-computing objects (e.g. Shapely Polygons), using `poly.buffer(0.0001)` on intersections can lead to negative or incorrect union areas if the unbuffered `poly.area` is used in the union calculation (`area_det + area_gt - area_inters`). This occurs prominently with degenerate polygons. Using `buffered_poly.area` resolves this inconsistency. Furthermore, degenerate geometric constructions often throw exceptions; these must be aggressively caught (`try...except Exception`) around object creations and operations to avoid halting the pipeline.
**Action:** When pre-computing buffered polygons, always pre-compute and store the *buffered* area alongside it to ensure geometric consistency in subsequent union calculations. Wrap all Shapeley geometry creations and intersections in `try...except Exception` to handle unexpected topological errors gracefully, providing default safe values.
