## 2025-05-19 - Fast Shapely Geometric Intersections
**Learning:** In PaddleOCR's `poly_nms`, repeatedly calling custom IOU logic that involves polygon buffering (e.g., `poly.buffer(0.0001)`) adds vertices and drastically slows down execution in O(N^2) loops. Pre-buffering polygons for intersections completely negates any caching benefits due to the extra vertices.
**Action:** When optimizing NMS loops with Shapely, pre-compute unbuffered polygons and their areas. Use `poly_A.intersection(poly_B)` instead of buffered intersections in inner loops. For highly irregular OCR polygons that may raise topological errors during intersection, wrap the call in a `try...except Exception:` block and gracefully fall back to the slower, unoptimized helper function (`boundary_iou`) to maintain exact backward compatibility.
