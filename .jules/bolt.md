## 2024-05-24 - Pre-compute Shapely Geometries in O(N) rather than O(N^2)
**Learning:** In highly nested, hot-loop operations such as `poly_nms` in PaddleOCR, executing Shapely geometry calculations natively inside the nested loops results in heavy redundant processing. It continuously rebuilds and re-buffers `Polygon` objects for every pairwise check, significantly dropping performance.
**Action:** When working with Shapely geometries in O(N^2) loops (like NMS or IOU comparison grids), pre-compute the `Polygon` objects and their buffered forms in O(N) beforehand. Wrap the pre-computation inside a `try...except Exception` block to catch errors from degenerate polygons, store `None`, and handle it safely during downstream IOU comparisons to retain safety. Inlining logic also removes function call overhead.
