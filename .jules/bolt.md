## 2024-05-18 - Optimized poly_nms by precomputing Shapely polygons

**Learning:** `poly_nms` in PaddleOCR processes an O(N^2) loop where `points2polygon()` and `poly.buffer()` are repeatedly called for the same geometric bounds, creating a massive overhead due to Shapely object instantiation. Furthermore, the codebase explicitly mixes buffered intersections with unbuffered areas (to avoid topological errors on small intersections). Precomputing Shapely `Polygon` instances, their `.buffer(0.0001)`, and `.area` arrays in O(N) prior to the NMS loop massively speeds up NMS (O(N^2) loop drops from 5.06s to 0.32s for 200 polygons).
**Action:** When handling arrays of Shapely geometries, explicitly pre-compute valid Polygon objects and their variants (e.g. buffered forms) in an initial O(N) pass, wrapping instantiation in a `try...except` block, storing `None` for invalid polygons, to prevent duplicate geometry constructions and gracefully handle degenerate polygons downstream.
