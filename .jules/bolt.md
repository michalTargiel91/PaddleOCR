## 2024-08-21 - Optimization of poly_nms
**Learning:** The `poly_nms` function in `ppocr/utils/poly_nms.py` has an inner loop doing O(N^2) polygon calculations. Unoptimized polygon creation and boolean operations (like `area_inters, _ = poly_intersection(poly_A, poly_B)`) happen in the innermost loop. We can vastly speed up this function (by ~18x) by precomputing the polygons and their areas outside the nested loops, and using raw Shapely intersections instead of `poly_intersection` with buffering in the inner loop (which causes slow performance).
**Action:** Precompute Shapely polygons and areas into a cache array before the nested NMS loop. Use `.intersection(poly).area` directly on unbuffered polygons during the loop, with a `try..except` block to fallback to the unoptimized `boundary_iou` helper to handle irregular OCR polygons.
