## 2024-05-18 - Optimize poly_nms and Fix Negative IoU Bug
**Learning:** In PaddleOCR, `poly_nms` performance suffers greatly due to O(N^2) redundant Shapely Polygon instantiations and geometric operations. Additionally, relying on `poly.area` (unbuffered area) for union calculations causes negative IoU and logic errors for degenerate polygons (e.g., flat lines), where the unbuffered area is zero but the buffered intersection area is non-zero. Pre-computing shapes in O(N) improves speed, but we must be careful with `poly_union` behavior.
**Action:** Pre-compute buffered polygons and their buffered areas (`buffered_poly.area`) in O(N), wrapped in `try...except` to catch `TopologicalError`. Inline intersection and union logic inside the hot loop to avoid function overhead, and use `buffered_poly.area` instead of unbuffered area to guarantee correct IoU logic.
