## 2024-05-18 - Precompute shapely polygons in O(N^2) NMS loop
**Learning:** `poly_nms` in PaddleOCR uses an O(N^2) loop where it repeatedly creates `Polygon` objects, computes their buffers, and calculates intersections and unions. Re-creating `shapely.geometry.Polygon` objects inside this tight O(N^2) inner loop is extremely expensive and causes massive bottlenecks (taking ~4.5s for just 200 boxes).
**Action:** Always extract shapely polygon creation and buffering out of the NMS nested loop. Precompute all `Polygon` and buffered `Polygon` objects beforehand in an O(N) loop. Then, during the O(N^2) loop, inline the IOU math to use the precomputed buffers (`poly_inter = poly_a_buf & poly_b_buf`) and precomputed unbuffered areas to exactly match the original fallback math behavior.
