## 2024-05-24 - Precomputing Shapely Intersections for NMS Optimization
**Learning:** Pre-buffering polygons for geometric intersections can be much faster when cached and done outside a tight NMS loop. In `poly_nms`, dynamically creating `points2polygon(A_raw)` and `points2polygon(B_raw)` thousands of times inside nested loops results in O(N^2) object creations and redundant `.buffer()` operations within `poly_intersection`. By precomputing the `.buffer(0.0001)` polygons and `.area` properties in a single pass O(N) at the start, the O(N^2) intersection step strictly performs Shapely topological operations, dramatically dropping loop overhead from ~76s to ~11s for 1000 polygons.
**Action:** Always inspect tightly nested NMS loops for redundant polygon/box instantiation. Precompute and cache geometric properties outside loops and try...except fallback to unoptimized helpers to handle topological errors smoothly without sacrificing the main fast-path optimization.
