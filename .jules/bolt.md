## 2024-05-24 - Precomputing Polygons in NMS\n**Learning:** Instantiating and buffering Shapely Polygon objects inside an O(N^2) Non-Maximum Suppression loop causes a significant performance bottleneck due to redundant geometric calculations. Also, older Shapely versions might throw TopologicalError, so geometric intersection should be wrapped in a try/except.\n**Action:** Always pre-compute base Polygons, their unbuffered areas, and their buffered representations in O(N) before the NMS loop, and wrap the intersection operation in a try...except returning 0.0 IoU on failure.
