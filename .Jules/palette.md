## 2024-05-18 - Maintain accessibility standards in example/test files
**Learning:** Test and example files (like `test_tipc/web/index.html`) often serve as references or boilerplate that other developers copy. Neglecting basic accessibility (like missing `alt` attributes on images) in these files can inadvertently propagate poor UX practices throughout the codebase or to external users.
**Action:** Always ensure basic accessibility standards (e.g., semantic tags, `alt` text, labels) are met even in non-production, test, or example environments.
